# ⛽ FuelMate

A mobile UI/UX prototype designed to help users estimate fuel costs, understand fuel consumption, and plan refueling during a trip.

## 📱 Overview

FuelMate is a mobile prototype created for a **Software Design** project. The concept focuses on making trip planning easier by helping users estimate how much fuel they may need, how much the trip may cost, and when they may need to refuel.

The interface was designed in **Figma** with a simple and intuitive mobile experience in mind.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Trip Planning** — Set up a trip and view relevant trip information.
- **Fuel Cost Estimation** — Estimate the fuel cost required for a trip.
- **Fuel Consumption** — View estimated fuel usage based on the trip.
- **Refueling Planning** — Estimate when refueling may be needed during a journey.
- **Map & Route View** — Visualize the trip route and related information.
- **Simple Mobile Interface** — Designed to keep the experience clear and easy to understand.

---

## 🎨 Design

FuelMate was designed as a mobile-first UI/UX prototype using **Figma**.

The design focuses on:

- Clear information hierarchy
- Simple navigation
- Easy-to-understand trip and fuel information
- Practical interactions for trip planning
